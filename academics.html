OUR ACADEMIC APPROACH

Education That Develops the Whole Learner

At Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf, education goes beyond classroom instruction. We aim to create meaningful learning experiences that help learners develop knowledge, practical skills, confidence and independence.

Our approach recognizes the diverse learning needs of students with hearing impairment and seeks to provide appropriate communication, instructional and learning support.
