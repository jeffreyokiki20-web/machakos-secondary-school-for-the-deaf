Academics

Supporting learning, skills development and academic growth

Academic Excellence

At Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf, education goes beyond classroom instruction. We aim to create meaningful learning experiences that help learners develop knowledge, practical skills, confidence and independence.


Our approach recognizes the diverse learning needs of students with hearing impairment and seeks to provide appropriate communication, instructional and learning support.

Our Approach to Learning

Inclusive Learning

We seek to provide learning experiences that recognize individual abilities and support meaningful participation.

Learner-Centred Teaching

Learners are encouraged to participate actively in the learning process and develop confidence in their abilities.

Practical Learning

Practical activities can help learners connect classroom knowledge with real-life situations.

Individual Support

Appropriate educational support can help learners overcome barriers and reach their potential.
