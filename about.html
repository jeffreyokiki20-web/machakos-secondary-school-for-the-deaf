About Our School

Learn more about Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf

Who We Are

Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf is committed to providing quality education and holistic development for learners with hearing impairment.


Our school seeks to create a supportive and inclusive environment where every learner can develop academically, socially, emotionally and practically.


We believe that every learner possesses unique abilities, talents and potential. Through dedicated teaching, guidance and appropriate learning support, we aim to empower our students to become confident and responsible members of society.

Our Vision

To develop confident, skilled and responsible learners who are prepared to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality, inclusive and learner-centred education that supports the academic, social and practical development of learners with hearing impairment.
