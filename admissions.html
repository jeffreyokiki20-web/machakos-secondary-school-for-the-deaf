Admissions

Information for prospective learners and families

Welcome to Our Admissions Section

Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf welcomes learners who meet the school's admission requirements and are seeking quality education in a supportive learning environment.


We encourage parents, guardians and prospective learners to contact the school directly for the latest information about available places, admission requirements, reporting dates and any required documentation.

Admission Information

Important areas to consider when applying

📝

Admission Requirements

Prospective learners should meet the applicable educational and school admission requirements.


Contact the school to confirm the current requirements before making arrangements.

📄

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to provide relevant educational and identification documents.


The school should be contacted for the current list of required documents.

📞

Enquiries

Parents and guardians are encouraged to contact the school for clarification about the admission process.

General Admission Process

1

Enquire

Contact the school to obtain current admission information.

2

Apply

Complete the applicable admission application process.

3

Assessment

Follow any assessment or verification procedures required by the school.

4

Reporting

Successful applicants receive information about reporting and joining procedures.

Information Parents and Guardians Should Confirm

  • Current admission requirements
  • Available places and entry levels
  • Required learner documentation
  • Reporting and admission dates
  • School fees and other approved charges
  • Boarding or accommodation arrangements, where applicable
  • Uniform and personal requirements
  • Any special educational or support requirements

Important Notice

The information on this page is intended as general guidance while the official school admission information is being prepared. Parents and guardians should confirm all admission, fee and reporting information directly with the school before making decisions.

Need More Information?

Contact Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf for official admission information and enquiries.
