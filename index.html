ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf

Machakos Secondary School for the Deaf is committed to providing quality education and holistic development for learners with hearing impairment.

We believe that every learner has unique abilities, talents and potential. Our school provides a supportive environment where students can learn, grow and prepare for a successful future.
